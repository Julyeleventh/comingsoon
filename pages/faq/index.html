Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about ordering, delivery and what to expect when your treats arrive.

Still have a question? Contact us and we will get back to you.

How do I place an order?

Catch us in person at markets and pop-ups around the GTA, and follow us on Instagram @july11to for our next stop. You can also order online: send us a DM with what you’d like, and we’ll confirm your order and send a secure Stripe payment request. A full online ordering system is coming soon.

What comes with my order?

Every product arrives with a card that lists the full product details and storage guidance, so you can serve it to your dog with confidence.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery is free on any order in Halton. In other GTA areas, delivery is free on orders over $80.

When do I need to order, and when will I receive it?

Halton delivery: order by Tuesday 12 pm for delivery on Wednesday after 6 pm, or by Friday 12 pm for delivery on Saturday.

Other GTA areas: order by Friday 12 pm for delivery on Sunday.

Pickup, 1160 King Rd, Burlington, ON: order by Tuesday 12 pm for pickup on Wednesday, 6 pm to 8 pm, or by Friday 12 pm for pickup on Saturday, 12 pm to 2 pm.

Need a specific time? Message us and we’ll do our best to accommodate.

How do you prepare your treats?

Everything is freshly made to order in small batches, so it reaches you fresh, within 24 hours of baking.

What is your return policy?

Because these are food products, we cannot accept returns. We take care to check the quality of every batch before it leaves our kitchen. If anything is not right, please contact us and we will look into it.
