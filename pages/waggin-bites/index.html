We Believe Every Dog Deserves The Best

We do things differently because every dog is worth the extra care.

Real Meat Dog Treats, Never Oily

We offer rabbit and chicken: pick the meat that suits your dog and we blend it with goat cheese, cooked through for a soft, rich texture that isn’t greasy. It’s a meat-first dog treat made with limited ingredients.

Why Choose Rabbit?

Freeze-Dried Fruit Dog Treats, Real Fruit Not Flavour

We choose strawberry, blueberry and pumpkin because they're fruits that are beneficial to dogs and that dogs love, and we mix them through every piece as real fruit, not fruit flavouring. Freeze-drying gently removes the water while keeping much of the fruit's original taste and nutrition, so what you see in each cut is the fruit itself.

Limited Ingredient Dog Treats With Nothing Hidden

Every ingredient is listed in full on the card that is attached to the package, from the meat and goat cheese to each fruit and plant powder. We want to make sure you can feed with peace of mind.

Fresh-Made Dog Treats, Never Sitting In A Warehouse

We don't warehouse our treats. Every batch is made fresh and never stored for months: the meat is cooked through, chilled for 12 hours, then dried gently for 12 more, so your treats reach you fresh from our kitchen.

Real Meat Leads The Base

We Will Launch Rabbit As Option For Sensitive Dogs

We know rabbit is hard to find and hard to reach, so we want to make rabbit available for all dogs. Now sensitive dogs can enjoy their treats like everyone else.

  • Plenty Of Protein, Little Fat

    Rabbit is packed with the protein dogs need to thrive, and it is a naturally lean meat: low in calories and cholesterol, which helps prevent obesity.

  • Promotes Muscle Development

    The amino acids in rabbit protein, such as isoleucine and valine, help repair tissue and support muscles.

  • Essential Vitamins And Coat Health

    Rabbit is a source of vitamins such as B12, which boosts blood cell growth and helps digestion, and its healthy fats keep a dog's coat sleek and skin healthy.

  • A Novel Protein Source

    Rabbit is less common in commercial dog food, and is often considered for dogs with sensitivities to common proteins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our treats, ordering and delivery.

How do I place an order?

Catch us in person at markets and pop-ups around the GTA, and follow us on Instagram @july11to for our next stop. You can also order online: send us a DM with what you’d like, and we’ll confirm your order and send a secure Stripe payment request. A full online ordering system is coming soon.

What comes with my order?

Every product arrives with a card that lists the full product details and storage guidance, so you can serve it to your dog with confidence.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery is free on any order in Halton. In other GTA areas, delivery is free on orders over $80.

When do I need to order, and when will I receive it?

Halton delivery: order by Tuesday 12 pm for delivery on Wednesday after 6 pm, or by Friday 12 pm for delivery on Saturday.

Other GTA areas: order by Friday 12 pm for delivery on Sunday.

Pickup, 1160 King Rd, Burlington, ON: order by Tuesday 12 pm for pickup on Wednesday, 6 pm to 8 pm, or by Friday 12 pm for pickup on Saturday, 12 pm to 2 pm.

Need a specific time? Message us and we’ll do our best to accommodate.

How do you prepare your treats?

Everything is freshly made to order in small batches, so it reaches you fresh, within 24 hours of baking.

What is your return policy?

Because these are food products, we cannot accept returns. We take care to check the quality of every batch before it leaves our kitchen. If anything is not right, please contact us and we will look into it.

Can Waggin’ Bites replace a meal?

No. They are treats, made to be given in moderation, and do not replace a complete and balanced diet.

How should I store them?

Each piece comes in its own custom-made individual wrapper.
